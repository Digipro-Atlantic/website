Financial institutions

Lend against rent, with the decision yours

Zebul routes pre-assessed rental loan applications to you and shares an advisory assessment built from on-platform records and, with consent, third-party data. You run your own credit decision, set your own terms, and disburse directly to the landlord. You hold the relationship and the risk. Zebul is a facilitator, not a lender.

  • A pipeline of pre-assessed applicants
  • Advisory assessments, advisory only
  • Direct disbursement to the landlord
  • Portfolio and repayment tools
  • Requires a valid CBN licence

Estate associations and developers

Bring your estates onto Zebul's governance tools at zero direct cost to committees or owners, and give every unit one place for dues and records.

Service providers
